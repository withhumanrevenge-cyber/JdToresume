# Phase 0 interview guide (20 interviews, ~30 min each)

**Goal:** learn how people tailor applications *today*, what it costs them, and whether they'd pay us to do it. Ask about **past behavior**, never hypotheticals ("would you use…?" gets polite lies). Show the product only in the last 5 minutes, if at all.

## Who (quota)
| Segment | Count | Where to find them |
|---------|-------|--------------------|
| India early-career (final year, 0–3 yrs) | 10 | Your college WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn #opentowork, placement-cell contacts, Telegram job channels |
| US new grads / international students (F-1/OPT) | 5 | Indian student associations at US universities, r/f1visa (read the rules first), alumni |
| Laid-off / mid-career tech | 5 | LinkedIn #opentowork posts, layoff community Slacks, personal network |

**Screener (all must be true):** actively applying now or in the last 60 days · applied to ≥10 roles · 18+.

## Script

**Warm-up (3 min)**
- "Tell me about your job search right now. What roles, how long, how's it going?"

**Current behavior (12 min)**
- "Walk me through the last application you sent, step by step. Where did you find the job? What did you do before clicking apply?"
- "Did you change your resume for it? What exactly did you change? How long did it take?"
- "How many applications did you send last week? How many did you tailor?"
- "Have you used ChatGPT, Claude or any resume tool for this? Show me (screen share) the last thing you did with it."
- "What went wrong with it?" (Listen for: made things up, sounded robotic, formatting broke, took too long, couldn't get a PDF.)
- "Have you written a cover letter or messaged a recruiter in the last month? How did that go?"

**Pain and stakes (7 min)**
- "What's the most frustrating part of applying right now?"
- "How many interviews have you had out of how many applications?" (Write both numbers down.)
- "Have you paid for anything in your job search: LinkedIn Premium, Naukri, a resume writer, a course? How much? Was it worth it?"

**Our test (5 min)**, only after the above:
- "If you could share a job link and get a tailored resume PDF, a cover letter and a recruiter message in 30 seconds, using only your real experience, what would you do with it?"
- **Commitment test** (the real signal): "We're running a 2-week pilot where we prepare these for 5 of your applications. It's [₹199 / $9] for the pilot, refundable. Want in?" Record yes / no / maybe. A yes means a payment link sent today.

**Close (3 min)**
- "Who else do you know who's job hunting right now?" (Referrals are a virality signal in themselves.)

## Synthesis (after every 5 interviews)
- Rank the pains by frequency and intensity, using quotes, not paraphrase.
- **Pass bar for Phase 0:** ≥ 8 of 20 say yes to the paid pilot, and ≥ 10 pre-orders or deposits overall (ROADMAP). Below that, change the segment or the offer before building more.
