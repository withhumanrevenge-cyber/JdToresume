## Task: write a cover letter for the job in <job_spec>, using the plan in <match_report>

250–350 words, three or four short paragraphs, first person, in the candidate's voice.
1. Hook (2–3 sentences): name the role and one specific thing from job_spec.company_signals or the responsibilities that genuinely connects to the candidate. Never open with "I am excited to apply" or similar.
2. Proof (one or two paragraphs): two short stories that map to the job's two most important basic requirements, each with a concrete result from the facts (with its number if the fact has one). Do not restate the resume line by line.
3. Fit: concrete and honest. If an important basic requirement is a gap, you may address it in one honest sentence about what the candidate has instead (for example an adjacent tool) and list its id in addressed_gap_requirement_ids. Never claim the missing skill.
4. Close: one clear, low-pressure ask (a short conversation).

- salutation: "Dear Hiring Team," unless the job spec names the hiring manager. Never invent a name.
- closing: "Best regards," — the name is added later.
- paragraphs contain only the body (no salutation, closing or signature).
- source_fact_ids: every fact the letter relies on.
