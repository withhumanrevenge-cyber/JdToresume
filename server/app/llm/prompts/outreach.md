## Task: write the outreach pack for the job in <job_spec>, using the plan in <match_report>

- recruiter_email_subject: specific — the role plus two or three of the strongest relevant credentials (for example "Backend Engineer, Payments: 5 yrs Java, high-volume APIs").
- recruiter_email_body: at most 120 words. Who the candidate is and which role → two proof points from the facts → the ask (a short call) → sign off with {{FULL_NAME}}. No other contact details.
- linkedin_note: at most 200 characters. The role, one specific hook, a soft ask. No referral request in a first message.
- referral_ask: at most 100 words, to someone the candidate knows at the company. Name the role and include the placeholder [job link], a two-line pitch, an offer to send the resume, and an easy way to say no.
- follow_up: at most 80 words, for 7–10 days after applying. Mention the role and the placeholder [application date], add one new relevant fact, restate interest.
- Never invent mutual connections, prior conversations or referrals.
- source_fact_ids: every fact used.
